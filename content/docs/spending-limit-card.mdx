## Usage

```tsx
import { SpendingLimitCard } from "@omnidotdev/thornberry/spending-limit-card";
```

```tsx
<SpendingLimitCard
  productName="Synapse"
  pausedUsage="requests above your plan's included allowance"
  value={limit}
  isLoading={isLoading}
  canEdit={isWorkspaceAdmin}
  onSave={({ hardLimitCents }) =>
    setSpendingLimit({ data: { organizationId, hardLimitCents } })
  }
/>
```

The card shows the overage spent this billing period against the monthly cap, a progress bar that turns destructive at 90%, when the period ends, and a paused notice once the cap is reached. Viewers with `canEdit` set, raise, or lower the cap; everyone else sees the spend and `readOnlyHint`.

There is no way to remove a cap: raising it is the only opt-in to more spend. A typed amount must be a plain dollar amount from `minCents` to `maxCents` ($10 to $100,000 by default). A cap at or below the spend so far pauses usage the moment it is saved, so the card asks for confirmation first.

The card is presentational. The product fetches `value` (null when its plan has no spending limit) and saves through `onSave`, which receives `{ hardLimitCents }` (plus `softLimitCents` with `withSoftLimit`). Resolve to close the form; reject with a user-safe `Error` to keep it open and show the message. Authorization stays on the server: `canEdit` only decides what the viewer is offered.

`pausedUsage` names what stops at the cap as a plural noun phrase ("your services", "requests above your plan's included allowance"). It completes the default description, the paused notice, and the confirmation.

## Examples

<br />

#### Paused

<div className="my-4">
  <SpendingLimitCard
    productName="Fractal"
    pausedUsage="your services"
    value={{
      hardLimitCents: 50000,
      currentSpendCents: 50000,
      suspended: true,
    }}
    canEdit={false}
    onSave={async () => {}}
  />
</div>

#### Row

`variant="row"` renders one product per line for a cross-product list, headed by the product name, with `meta` under it and `action` (e.g. a link to the product's billing page) beside the edit button.

<div className="my-4">
  <SpendingLimitCard
    variant="row"
    productName="Synapse"
    meta="Pro plan"
    pausedUsage="requests above your plan's included allowance"
    value={{ hardLimitCents: 10000, currentSpendCents: 4250, suspended: false }}
    canEdit
    onSave={async () => {}}
  />
</div>

#### Alert threshold

`withSoftLimit` adds an optional "Alert email at" amount below the cap, for products whose billing sends an extra alert there.
