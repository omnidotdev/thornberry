`ConfirmDialog` covers what products kept local copies for: `error` shows a failed attempt's message above the actions, `pendingLabel` labels the in-flight confirm button (e.g. "Deleting..."), and `confirmVariant` styles the confirm button for confirmations that are irreversible but not destructive. It now renders with the `alertdialog` role.
